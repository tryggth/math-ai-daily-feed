### 2. Theorem Proving & Reasoning Architectures

* **[Resolution and Lean 4 Formalization of the Morgan-Mullen Conjecture]**  
  [Resolvent Components of Completely Normal Elements and the Morgan-Mullen Conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36534v1) — Florian Hellwig  
  - **Mathematical Impact:** (Score: 5 — Landmark Breakthrough). Conclusively settles the long-standing Morgan-Mullen Conjecture (1996) by proving that every finite field extension $\mathbb{F}_{q^n}$ over $\mathbb{F}_q$ contains a primitive element that is completely normal. The mathematical arguments for $n > q$ are fully formalized in Lean 4 with Mathlib, while the remaining 70 exceptional pairs $(q,n)$ are settled via verified computation.
  - **AI Connection:** Interactive theorem prover formalization in Lean 4 with Mathlib, utilizing Lean's native compiler and interpreter for computational verification of 70 exceptional pairs $(q,n)$ (no neural AI).

* **[Independence of Global Choice from Excluded Middle in Dependent Type Theory]**  
  [Global Choice without Excluded Middle in Dependent Type Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37498v1) — Sewon Park  
  - **Mathematical Impact:** (Score: 4 — Substantive Mathematical Contribution). Resolves a fundamental foundational question in constructive type theory and proof assistants (such as Lean) by constructing a consistent staged-set model of extensional Martin-Löf type theory with global choice that refutes both the Law of Excluded Middle and Propositional Extensionality.
  - **AI Connection:** Classical symbolic computation / proof theory (no neural AI).

* **[count-catcher: Verifying Combinatorial Model Counts in Lean 4]**  
  [count-catcher: Lean Theorems from Model Counting Certificates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36196v1) — Johannes K. Fichte, Stefan Szeider  
  - **Mathematical Impact:** (Score: 4 — Substantive Mathematical Contribution). Establishes an automated tool (`count-catcher`) that converts CPOG certificates generated by exact model counters into formally verified Lean 4 theorems, enabling machine-checked proofs of combinatorial counts including the number of Latin squares of order 8.
  - **AI Connection:** Classical symbolic computation / exact model counter (`d4` producing CPOG certificates) verified via LRAT refutations in Lean 4 (no neural AI).

* **[Counterexample and Complete Calculus for KL1 Action Logic]**  
  [A Counterexample to KL1 Completeness and a Complete Disjunctive Calculus](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37107v1) — Damiano Presa, Fabio Aurelio D'Asaro, Massimiliano Badino  
  - **Mathematical Impact:** (Score: 4 — Substantive Mathematical Contribution). Disproves the completeness conjecture for the normative logic of action KL1 via an explicit counterexample, formulates a complete disjunctive rule generalization (`may-trans-disj`), and machine-verifies the formal meta-logical results in Agda.
  - **AI Connection:** Interactive theorem prover formalization in Agda (no neural AI).
