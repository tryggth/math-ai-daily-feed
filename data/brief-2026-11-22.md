* **[Breaking the Prime-Gap Barrier for Symmetric Boolean Functions]**  
  [Degree Lower Bounds for Symmetric Boolean Functions: Breaking the Prime-Gap Barrier](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26446v1) — Xiaoming Sun, Chengu Wang  
  - **Mathematical Impact:** (Score: 4 — Substantive Mathematical Contribution). Advances progress toward von zur Gathen and Roche's conjecture by establishing that every nonconstant symmetric Boolean function on $n$ variables has exact real polynomial degree at least $n - \widetilde{O}(n^{1/3})$, surpassing the traditional $O(n^{0.525})$ limit of prime-gap methods without relying on number-theoretic conjectures.  
  - **AI Connection:** Interactive theorem prover Lean 4; every numbered result in the paper has been formally verified in Lean 4.
